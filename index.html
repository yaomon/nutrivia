which formats work?
CSV / TSV — one question per row, correct answer last:
What is 2+2?,3,4,5,6,B
(a header row like question,a,b,c,d,answer also works)
Aiken — the Moodle plain-text format:
What is 2+2?
A. 3
B. 4
ANSWER: B
GIFT — Moodle's other format:
What is 2+2? {=4 ~3 ~5}
JSON — an array of objects with a question, options and an answer.

How to Play

🧠 Answer right to earn 💙 XP and 💰 gold. Streaks 🔥 and fast answers ⚡ earn bonus XP.
❤️ Wrong answers cost 5 HP. When HP runs out, the run ends.
🎁 Fill the XP bar to level up: pick a prize, then discover a new food every single time.
🎒 Your items live at the bottom left. Tap one to see what it does, tap it again to use it.
🛒 Gold banks between runs — spend it in the Pantry Shop to stock items for your next run.
