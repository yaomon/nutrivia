How to Play

🧠 Answer right to earn 💙 XP and 💰 gold. Streaks 🔥 and fast answers ⚡ earn bonus XP.

🎁 Fill the XP bar to level up: pick a prize, then discover a new food every single time.
🎒 Your items live at the bottom left. Tap one to see what it does, tap it again to use it.
🛒 Gold banks between runs — spend it in the Pantry Shop to stock items for your next run.
